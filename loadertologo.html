Loader to Logo

A single SVG path pulses square–circle while the page loads, then morphs straight into the conomica mark and flies into place as the wordmark wipes in.

Loading

flubber didn't load — the mark snaps in without the morph.

The loader is one <path>. While loading it oscillates square–circle; when content is ready, flubber interpolates that shape straight into the mark's outline over ~0.85s, while a CSS transform carries it from the centre of the viewport into its slot in the logo. The wordmark then wipes in through an animated <clipPath>.

To wire it up: run the oscillation on page load, and call the resolve step from your load event (or whenever your data is in). Everything lives in runLoader() below — about 30 lines.
